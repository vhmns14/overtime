Brewed slow, on purpose

Kopi Senja started as a cart outside a bookshop. Today it is twelve seats, one roaster and a long counter facing west, so every cup comes with the sunset.

  • 3 origins on rotation
  • 12 seats, no rush
  • 48h from roast to cup
